<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Moving between the tabs and between pages feels smooth and consistent. No new visual element is drawn (the developer's pictures have none for this): the bar keeps its look, and what moves is its state, the screens under it, and the pages pushed over them.

Out of scope: the spin wheel's own motion (Phase 2 UI spec section 5), the entry reveals of Phases 3 to 6 (their specs), sound, haptics, shared-element expansions between a list row and its detail page (a deferred option below).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Decisions (defaults taken 2026-10-13; every one is a paper edit to change)

- **D-01 Reading of the request.** The developer wants the tab bar and the screens to move smoothly instead of swapping instantly. Their own direction document says "do not animate the entire interface every time the user changes tabs; subtle active-state transitions, small icon and label movement, gentle opacity changes, no bouncing icons" (section 8). Both are honoured: the screens fade into each other, the bar's active state glides, nothing slides across the screen and nothing bounces. The developer's words of 2026-10-13 win where they differ from section 8; the resolution is recorded there.
- **D-02 Tab switch: a cross-fade.** The screen under the bar fades into the next one, 200ms, ease-out (`animation: 'fade'` on the Tabs screens). Tabs keep their scroll position and loaded data (they stay mounted). With Reduce Motion on, the swap is instant.
- **D-03 The bar's active state glides.** The icon and the label of the item that becomes active change colour from `muted` to `ink` over 220ms, and the one that stops being active goes back over the same time (an interpolated colour, not a jump). The pressed item compresses to 0.97 over 90ms and settles back (the existing press feel). Outline icons stay outline (as Phase 2's spec and the pictures); no bounce, no new weight change that would shift the label.
- **D-04 The Spin button** keeps its two looks (Phase 2) and its press feel. Pressing it goes to Home as before; Home's screen fades in like any tab. The button's fill still changes at once when the server's state changes (Phase 2 section 8: no animation between its looks), so nothing on it is animated by this phase except the shared press feel.
- **D-05 Pushed pages** (Edit wheel, Settings, the entry page, the post page, the person page, Notifications) keep the platform's own push: a smooth slide in from the right with the edge swipe to go back (iOS). No custom page animation is added. Sheets keep Phase 2's `ConfirmSheet` motion (slide up, scrim fade).
- **D-06 Lists** reveal rows once, the first time a screen shows data (the existing `FadeInUp`, staggered by 60 to 90ms, at most the first eight rows). Coming back to a tab never replays it. Pull to refresh does not replay it.
- **D-07 One set of numbers**, in one file (`lib/motion/navigation.ts`): press 90ms, tab fade 200ms, bar colour 220ms, content rise 400ms (the existing `FadeInUp`), image reveal 450ms (the existing `ImageReveal`), the same ease-out curve everywhere. No screen invents its own.
- **D-08 Reduce Motion** (the phone's accessibility setting, read through Reanimated's reduced-motion support): tab switch instant, bar colours change at once, lists appear at once, the press feel stays (it is feedback, 90ms) unless the phone says otherwise. Same rule as every earlier phase.
- **D-09 No new native module, no rebuild.** The installed navigation (expo-router 57) already offers `animation: 'none' | 'fade' | 'shift'`, a `transitionSpec` and a custom `sceneStyleInterpolator` on the tab screens, and Reanimated 4.5.1 is installed. Never run `expo lint`.

### Options the developer can choose with one word (not built unless asked)
- **"slide":** tab screens shift a little left or right in the direction of the tab (`animation: 'shift'`). More movement, closer to "moving around", further from section 8.
- **"line":** a thin dark line under the active tab glides between items. It is a NEW visual element that is not in the pictures, so it is off unless asked.
- **"expand":** a shared-element expansion from a diary row or a Board card into its page ("if technically practical", section 6 of the motion document). It needs more than the installed tools do reliably, so it is deferred; the default is the platform push plus a gentle fade-in of the page's content.

### The check (the developer only; a screenshot cannot show smoothness)
Open the app, tap Home, Diary, Board, Profile in turn, and back. It should feel like one calm fade each time, the bar's colours should melt rather than blink, and nothing should flash white, jump, or replay a list. Then turn Reduce Motion on in iOS Settings and see that the same taps swap at once.

</decisions>
