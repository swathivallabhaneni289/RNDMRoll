<binding_decisions>
Final.
1. The contract is `02.2-CONTEXT.md` D-01 to D-09 exactly. The bar's look (fill, border, height, icons, labels, the Spin button) is Phase 2's and does not change.
2. One file of numbers: `lib/motion/navigation.ts` exports `pressMs` 90, `tabFadeMs` 200, `barColorMs` 220 and the existing ease-out curve. No literal duration appears in `_layout.tsx` or the new components.
3. The cross-fade is the Tabs option `animation: 'fade'` with a `transitionSpec` of `tabFadeMs` and the ease-out curve, set once in `screenOptions`. With Reduce Motion on it is `'none'`.
4. The active state glide is drawn by two small components used through `tabBarIcon` and `tabBarLabel`: `TabIcon` and `TabLabel` take `focused` and animate a colour between `color.muted` and `color.ink` with a shared value and `interpolateColor` over `barColorMs`. Same icon names, same size (24) and label style as today, so no layout shift.
5. The Spin button is not touched (its two looks and press feel are Phase 2's).
6. Hidden-bar routes (`display: none`) keep working: the glide never runs on a bar that is not drawn.
</binding_decisions>
