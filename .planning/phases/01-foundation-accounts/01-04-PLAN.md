<objective>
Build the reusable visual layer for all nine Phase 1 screens: the two brand vector components and the twelve UI primitives, each wired directly to the UI-SPEC revision 9 tokens from plan 01-02.

Purpose: UI-SPEC revision 9 is precise about elevation tiers, border opacity steps, radius caps, icon-badge fill rules, the scrim's opaque-zone contrast guarantee, and the photo placeholder treatment. Encoding each rule once in a component is what makes nine screens obey them without nine independent chances to drift.

Scope added by UI-SPEC revision 9 (the pre-signup marketing sequence and the consolidated create-profile screen, per D-05 revised 2026-09-17): the numeric step indicator that replaces the retired dot indicator, the photo placeholder and its three panel geometries, the dual-edge Ink scrim, the on-photo CTA variant, and the swipe-plus-chevron advance control. Every one of those is a presentational component with no network, storage, or navigation logic, which is why this plan carries four tasks rather than three without exceeding its context budget.

Output: `components/brand/` and `components/ui/` trees that typecheck and enforce the design contract structurally.
</objective>

`components/ui/PhotoPanel.tsx` exports three non-interactive geometries, each filling itself with `<PhotoPlaceholder variant="panel" />` rather than declaring a fill of its own, and each combining `borderRadius: radius.lg` with the shadow keys of `elevation.card` (`shadowColor`, `shadowOffset`, `shadowOpacity`, `shadowRadius`, and the Android `elevation` number) while deliberately NOT taking that tier's white `backgroundColor`. UI-SPEC is explicit that these are photo stand-ins rather than white card surfaces, and that the recessed Secondary tone reads more correctly as an empty image than pure white would.
- `PhotoPanel({ aspectRatio = 4 / 5 })`: one panel, full width inside `space.lg` side margins, portrait-leaning by default.
- `PolaroidCard({ rotation })`: a square photo area with an additional `space.md` bottom padding strip inside the same card, simulating a polaroid frame's wider bottom border, carrying `transform: [{ rotate: rotation + 'deg' }]`. `rotation` is a required number supplied by the caller. Never randomize it and never animate it: PROJECT.md bans over-the-top scroll animations and UI-SPEC fixes the three values the caller passes.
- `SplitPhotoPanels()`: a `flexDirection: 'row'` container inside `space.lg` side margins holding exactly two `PhotoPanel`s, each `flex: 1`, separated by a `space.sm` gap.
None of the three accepts a press handler and none renders a touchable. UI-SPEC's rule for the 24dp token is that it is never applied to anything tappable, so a tappable panel would be a radius-cap violation rather than a legitimate use of the token.

`components/ui/PhotoScrim.tsx` exports `PhotoScrim({ topOpaqueFraction = SCRIM_TOP_SAFE_FRACTION })` plus the three constants `SCRIM_TOP_SAFE_FRACTION`, `SCRIM_BOTTOM_SAFE_FRACTION`, and `SCRIM_TOP_FADE_DEPTH`, set to `0.08`, `0.28`, and `0.12`. The component is absolutely positioned, `pointerEvents="none"`, fills its parent, and is rendered above the cover placeholder and below screen content. Build it with `react-native-svg`, which plan 01-02 already installed and Task 1 already uses; do NOT add a gradient package, since none is in the approved dependency set and adding one would require a fresh package legitimacy gate for a shape this library already draws. Render one `<Svg>` containing `<Defs>` with a single vertical `<LinearGradient id="scrim" x1="0" y1="0" x2="0" y2="1">` and a `<Rect width="100%" height="100%" fill="url(#scrim)" />`.

The gradient holds exactly six `<Stop>` elements, every one with `stopColor={color.ink}`, at these offsets and stop-opacities: `0`/`1`, `topOpaqueFraction`/`1`, `topOpaqueFraction + SCRIM_TOP_FADE_DEPTH`/`0`, `0.45`/`0`, `0.72`/`1`, `1`/`1`. At the default that evaluates to exactly UI-SPEC's declared dual-edge figures: the top flat zone runs 0 to 8 percent of height and fades out by 20 percent, and the bottom flat zone is the last 28 percent, which is offset 0.72 measured from the top, with its fade beginning at 55 percent measured up from the bottom, which is offset 0.45.

The top zone is the one parameterized figure, and the reason is a conflict the executor will otherwise hit and improvise around. UI-SPEC requires the cover screen's tagline to sit inside the flat top zone, and separately the tagline must clear the status bar, whose safe-area inset on a notched device is around 59dp. Two lines of Label type add about 40dp, while 8 percent of a typical 852dp screen is only about 68dp, so the declared fraction cannot contain content that also clears the inset. UI-SPEC's own framing is that the scrim is a guarantee mechanism rather than an assumption, and the guarantee is that every text element sits on 100-percent-opaque Ink; the percentage is the mechanism serving it. The exported constants exist so the cover screen positions its content from the same numbers the gradient is built from rather than retyping them. Because both flat zones are fully opaque Ink rather than a translucent blend, content placed inside them inherits the already-verified Dominant-on-Ink contrast ratio no matter what photograph eventually sits beneath.
