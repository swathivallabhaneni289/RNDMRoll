**A. The pre-signup marketing sequence, new in UI-SPEC revision 9**

Start from a genuinely clean install, or clear app data, so the intro flag is unset.

1. Launch. Confirm you land on the Welcome cover, not on the method chooser: a full-bleed panel, `one roll. / one real moment.` top-left, `RNDMRoll` and `Get started` anchored at the bottom. Confirm all three read cleanly against the dark scrim with no washed-out or half-transparent edge, and that the top tagline clears the status bar rather than colliding with the clock.
2. Confirm the `Get started` button is light-filled with dark text, which is the inverted on-photo variant, and is rectangular rather than pill-shaped. Every other primary button in the app is the opposite way round; this is the one screen where it inverts.
3. Tap `Get started`. Confirm you advance to The Ritual, reading `life's better when it's random.` with `01 / 03` shown as digits, not as a row of dots. Confirm the body says the wheel reveals **your** category and does not claim everyone gets the same one.
4. Advance by swiping left, then go back with the platform back gesture, then advance again by tapping the chevron instead. Both paths must work, and the chevron must be visible without discovering the swipe.
5. Continue through Real > Perfect (`02 / 03`) to Everyone Rolls (`03 / 03`). Confirm Everyone Rolls reads `everyone rolls. everyone shares.` and that its body describes each person rolling their own category from their own wheel.
6. Tap `Continue` and confirm you arrive at the choose-method screen.
7. Force quit and relaunch. Confirm you now land straight on choose-method and are **not** shown the four marketing screens again, and that there is no flash of the Welcome screen before choose-method appears. That flag is the only thing standing between a returning user and a repeated pitch.

**B. Email signup, the rest of the D-05 (revised) sequence**
8. On the choose-method screen, confirm the five-pip mark and the RNDMRoll wordmark sit above three buttons, the dot-grid texture is visible and static, and there is no phone number option anywhere. Confirm the dot grid appears here and on none of the four marketing screens.
9. Tap Continue with Email, create an account, and confirm you land on Check your email naming the address you typed.
10. Tap Resend email and confirm it disables with a 30 second countdown.
11. Open the verification link. With `MAIL_DRIVER=log` it prints to the server console; paste it into the simulator. Confirm you see a brief Verified state and are advanced automatically, with no extra Continue tap.
12. Confirm you land on **one** screen headed `Create your profile` carrying the avatar, name, username, and bio together. There must be no separate name screen, no separate username screen, and no separate photo screen.
13. Enter a name. Confirm the username field is **already filled** with a suggestion derived from it, never blank, and is editable. Watch the bio field below it while the status changes from checking to available: it must not jump up or down.
14. Type a username you know is taken. Confirm it shows Checking, then the taken message with up to three tappable alternates. Tap one and confirm it fills and rechecks.
15. Confirm the avatar is the largest element on the screen, that `Optional` sits beside it, and that there is **no** separate skip button. Finish setup once without ever touching the avatar and confirm it is not blocked. Then repeat the signup and add a photo from the camera and again from the library.

**C. Stay logged in, which is ACCT-01's distinguishing clause**
16. Force quit the app completely and relaunch. Confirm you land straight in the app with no login prompt, no visible flash of the signup screen, and no flash of the Welcome cover.

**D. Profile, which is ACCT-03**
17. Open the profile. Confirm it shows only avatar, name, username, and bio. There must be no streak, no roll count, and no diary total, not even as a placeholder. With no bio set, confirm the Add a bio prompt appears with its pencil badge and that tapping the row opens the editor.
18. Edit each of the four fields in turn, save, force quit, relaunch, and confirm all four persisted. Then log out and confirm the sheet reads "Log out of RNDMRoll? You'll need to sign back in." with Log out and Stay logged in, and that confirming returns you to the **method chooser**, not to the Welcome cover, since you have already seen the intro.

**E. Social sign-in**
19. On iOS, confirm Continue with Apple appears; on Android, confirm it does not and only Email and Google show. Sign in with Apple using an Apple ID that has never authorized this app, and confirm your name is captured and prefills the name field on the create-profile screen. **Then sign out and sign in with Apple again.** Apple returns the name only on the very first authorization and null every time after, so this second pass is the only way to catch a regression where the stored name gets overwritten with nothing. Confirm your name is still intact.
20. Sign in with Google and confirm the account is created already verified, going straight to the create-profile screen with no verification email.
