**A. Email signup, the full D-05 sequence**
1. Launch to the choose-method screen. Confirm the five-pip mark and the RNDMRoll wordmark sit above three buttons, the dot-grid texture is visible and static, and there is no phone number option anywhere.
2. Tap Continue with Email, create an account, and confirm you land on Check your email naming the address you typed.
3. Tap Resend email and confirm it disables with a 30 second countdown.
4. Open the verification link. With `MAIL_DRIVER=log` it prints to the server console; paste it into the simulator. Confirm you see a brief Verified state and are advanced automatically, with no extra Continue tap.
5. Enter a name. Confirm the username step arrives **already filled** with a suggestion derived from it, never blank, and is editable.
6. Type a username you know is taken. Confirm it shows Checking, then the taken message with up to three tappable alternates. Tap one and confirm it fills and rechecks.
7. On the photo step, add a photo from the camera and again from the library, then continue. Confirm Finish setup is the dominant button and Skip for now sits below it as plain text.

**B. Stay logged in, which is ACCT-01's distinguishing clause**
8. Force quit the app completely and relaunch. Confirm you land straight in the app with no login prompt and no visible flash of the signup screen.

**C. Profile, which is ACCT-03**
9. Open the profile. Confirm it shows only avatar, name, username, and bio. There must be no streak, no roll count, and no diary total, not even as a placeholder.
10. With no bio set, confirm the Add a bio prompt appears with its pencil badge and that tapping the row opens the editor.
11. Edit each of the four fields in turn, save, force quit, relaunch, and confirm all four persisted.
12. Log out. Confirm the sheet reads "Log out of RNDMRoll? You'll need to sign back in." with Log out and Stay logged in, and that confirming returns you to the method chooser.

**D. Social sign-in**
13. On iOS, confirm Continue with Apple appears; on Android, confirm it does not and only Email and Google show.
14. Sign in with Apple using an Apple ID that has never authorized this app. Confirm your name is captured and prefills the name step.
15. **Then sign out and sign in with Apple again.** Apple returns the name only on the very first authorization and null every time after, so this second pass is the only way to catch a regression where the stored name gets overwritten with nothing. Confirm your name is still intact.
16. Sign in with Google and confirm the account is created already verified, going straight to the name step with no verification email.

**E. Copy approval, the one decision this checkpoint needs from you**

- The email signup and login screen, an eighth screen with no UI-SPEC entry at all: heading `Sign up with email` toggling to `Log in`, the helper line `At least 8 characters.`, and the button `Create account`

Two deliberate omissions to confirm you agree with:
- **No Forgot password flow and no Forgot password link.** UI-SPEC mentions the link only in a colour usage list, not among its seven screens; CONTEXT.md leaves reset mechanics as undiscussed discretion; and neither ACCT-01 nor ACCT-03 requires it. Rendering a link with nothing behind it would be worse than omitting both, so both are omitted. Say the word and I will scope it as a follow-up.
- **No pre-signup marketing sequence.** The 2026-09-16 design brief describes a splash and three marketing screens. UI-SPEC's Scope note leaves that open and explicitly does not add it to this phase, so it is not built here.
