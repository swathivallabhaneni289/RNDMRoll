## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-CFG-01 | Information Disclosure | `.env` file at repo root | high | mitigate | `.gitignore` excludes `.env` and `.env.local` while tracking `.env.example`; Task 1 acceptance criteria greps for the `.env` ignore rule |

| T-01-CFG-04 | Information Disclosure | credentials pasted into the checkpoint transcript | low | accept | Single-developer local workflow; credentials go into `.env` directly, not into chat. Documented in the checkpoint wording. |
