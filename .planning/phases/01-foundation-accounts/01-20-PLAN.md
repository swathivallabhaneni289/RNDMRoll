# 01-20: photo and bio on their own page after sign-up

## Why

On 2026-10-08, during the 01-19 walkthrough, the developer asked for the bio and photo to sit on a different page after the signing up is done. They then asked how Instagram handles it. What could be verified: the 2022 iPhone sign-up had the profile photo as its own page after the account details (name, password, birthday, username); the 2024 Android recording went straight into the app with no photo page; neither asks for a bio at sign-up. The developer picked option a (a new page with just photo and bio, shown once after sign-up). Name and username stay on page 1 (the developer said "bio and photo"; no one objected).

## Design

- **Page 1, "Create your account."** (ProfileForm modes `signup` and `finish`): Heading role, no sub-line. Signup: Email, Password, Birthday, Name, Username, Continue with an arrow, "Log in instead". Finish (Apple or Google, unfinished): Birthday, Name, Username, Continue, Log out. No photo, no bio. The account is made by this page's Continue, in one request, as in 01-19.
- **Page 2, "Make it yours."** (new mode `extras`): the edit page without Name and Username. Camera circle with "Add photo", Bio (Optional), Continue with an arrow, and a "Skip for now" text link. No Back, no Log out. Both fields are optional, so Continue with nothing filled in just moves on. A photo uploads first; if that fails the person stays on the page with the message under the circle and nothing is saved, then retries or skips. Otherwise one save (bio and avatar), then the landing page.
- **After page 2** the landing page is the unchanged edit page ("Make it yours." with photo, name, username, bio, Save changes, Log out).
- Heading roles: Log in and Create your account. use Heading (22); "Make it yours." keeps Display (40), as in the developer's mockup. "Create your account." at Display size would wrap to two lines.

## How (no gated routing file is edited)

- `lib/session/store.ts`: an in-memory flag `extrasPending`. `signIn(result, { extras: true })` sets it before the status flips (a plain log in or a social sign-in clears it); `startExtras()` and `finishExtras()` set and clear it; sign-out clears it. A relaunch starts with it off.
- `app/(app)/profile/index.tsx` (the landing route every sign-in already reaches through the existing redirect) renders `ProfileForm` in mode `extras` while the flag is set and `edit` after, with `key={mode}` so nothing typed on one page carries to the other. `edit.tsx` stays.
- `ProfileForm`: `submitSignup` sends no bio and no photo and calls `signIn(result, { extras: true })`. `submitFinish` sends the birthday alone first (unchanged), then name and username, then `startExtras()` before `reloadUser()`. `submitExtras` uploads the photo (a failure sets the avatar message and returns), then PATCH /me with bio and avatar_url if either exists, `reloadUser()`, `finishExtras()`. Skip for now calls `finishExtras()`. The old photo-failed Alert is gone.
- Server: unchanged (bio was already optional on sign-up; the avatar upload already uses the signed-in token).
