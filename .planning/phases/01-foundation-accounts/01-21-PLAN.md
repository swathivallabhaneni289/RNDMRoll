# 01-21: a date scroller for the birthday, and green "Looks good." notes

## Why

On 2026-10-08, after Group 2 of the walkthrough passed with the typed boxes, the developer asked for less typing ("nobody wants to physically enter something"). Claude offered five picks (a birthday scroller, a keyboard Next key, hints inside the boxes, one-tap username choices, Apple and Google first). The developer chose: the birthday scroller, plus the green "looks good" notes (which Claude had listed as "not now"), applied to every field of the sign-up page and to the Apple and Google finish page, which shares the form. Declined: the other four picks, and Face ID ("not yet").

## Design

- **Birthday (iOS).** One soft box under the BIRTHDAY label: "Select your birthday" in Muted until a date is chosen, then the date ("April 15, 2000") with a small down chevron. Tap it: a bottom sheet (the log-out sheet's shape) with iOS's own scrolling date picker (month, day, year columns, drawn by the system) and a Done button. Done stays greyed until the picker has moved: the starting date (January 1, 25 years ago) is only a guess and the birthday cannot be changed after sign-up. The picker cannot go past today. The dark area above the sheet closes it without changing anything.
- **Rules unchanged.** The date goes through the same `checkBirthday` as typed boxes: under 13 shows "You must be at least 13 to use RNDMRoll." (sign-up); the finish page leaves the 13+ rule to the server so an under-13 account is refused and deleted there.
- **Off iOS** (or if the native view is missing) the three typed boxes stay, with their messages.
- **Green notes.** A green check and "Looks good." (the look of the username's "Available") under: Email as you type once it looks like an email; Password as you type once it has 8 or more characters (replacing the grey "At least 8 characters." line); Name after you leave the box, or at once when it came filled in; Birthday once a valid date is chosen, judged with the 13+ rule even on the finish page (a too-young date there shows neither note nor error). The username keeps "Available". An error always replaces the note. Sign-up and finish pages only; the edit page and the photo-and-bio page are unchanged.

## How

- `components/profile/BirthdayPickerField.tsx` (new): the row, the sheet (a Modal), and the SwiftUI `DatePicker` from `@expo/ui/swift-ui` (style wheel, inside `Host`, fixed height 216, light color scheme). The native pieces are loaded with `require` inside try/catch behind `Platform.OS === 'ios'`; `birthdayPickerAvailable` tells the form which version to draw. Dates are built at local noon and read back by LOCAL calendar parts. `range` runs from 1 January 1900 to now. `onDateChange` fires only when the person scrolls (read in `ios/DatePickerView.swift`), which is what lights up Done.
- `components/ui/TextField.tsx`: a `success` prop and an exported `FieldSuccess` (the green row), shown only when there is no error and the field's status is idle.
- `components/profile/ProfileForm.tsx`: the birthday block draws `BirthdayPickerField` when available, else the old boxes (which also get the note); `handleBirthdayPicked` fills the three parts from the chosen date and runs `checkBirthday`; `emailOk`, `passwordOk`, `nameOk` (with `nameTouched`) and `birthdayOk` drive the notes.
- The 24dp radius token is now also used in `BirthdayPickerField.tsx` (the date sheet only); the allow-list in 01-15-PLAN.md and UI-SPEC revision 15 says so.
