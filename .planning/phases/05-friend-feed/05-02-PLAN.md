<binding_decisions>
Final.
1. **No migration.** Part B of `0007_friend_feed` (written in plan 05-01 Task 2a) holds the tables. For reference, the contract this plan relies on:
   - `post_reactions (entry_id references entries(id) on delete cascade, user_id references users(id) on delete cascade, reaction text not null check in the same list as entries_reaction_check, created_at, updated_at, primary key (entry_id, user_id))`, indexes `(user_id, updated_at desc)` and `(entry_id, updated_at desc)`.
   - `post_comments (id uuid pk, entry_id references entries(id) on delete cascade, user_id references users(id) on delete cascade, body text not null check char_length(btrim(body)) between 1 and 140, created_at)`, indexes `(entry_id, created_at, id)` and `(user_id, created_at desc)`.
   All timestamps are written from Go (the actor's clock), no defaults.
2. **Day in play** (`spin.DayInPlay`, pure): let `d` be the local date of `now` in the viewer's stored zone and `opens` the 20:00 instant of `d` (`WindowFor`). If `now >= opens` the day in play is `d`; otherwise it is `PrevDate(d)` and `LateWindow` is true. From the Phase 2 clock only; no `time.Now()` outside the spin slice's clock.
3. **`spin.Service.BoardFacts(ctx, userID)`** is the only way the board learns anything about the viewer's own spins, their clock and their zone. It runs under the same lock, zone re-read and single `now` as `Today`, and returns `{Now, Zone, DayInPlay, LateWindow, Posted, Skipped, Action, CategoryName}` where: `Zone` is the viewer's stored zone (`*time.Location`, the one the time labels use); `Posted` is a `posted_days` row (any source) for `DayInPlay`; `Skipped` is "not `Posted` and the date's latest spin has result `skipped`"; `Action` is `log` when the date's latest spin exists, is still `open` and `now` is before that date's `late_until` (then `CategoryName` is its name as written), `spin` when Phase 2's own Today rule would say `open` (the same builder is called, not copied), else `wait`. The post routes read it too (for `Now` and `Zone`), so a person with no stored zone gets the same 400 field `time_zone` as on Today.
4. **Board states** (one pure function, `board.Decide`), checked in this order: no accepted friends is `no_friends`; `Posted` is `unlocked`; `Skipped` is `skipped`; else `locked`. The gate reads `posted_days` only.
5. **Which days are listed.** The gate is per day. In `unlocked`, `locked` and `skipped` alike: the viewer's posted dates, newest first, no later than the day in play, paged by `before` (exclusive) and `limit` (1 to 10, default 5). In `locked` and `skipped` the viewer has no posted day for the day in play (that is why they are locked or skipped), so the list is exactly the EARLIER days the viewer posted and is empty when there are none; in `unlocked` the day in play comes first when posted. For each date: the entries on that `local_date` by the viewer and by accepted friends, newest first by `created_at`, then id. A date with no cards is left out of `days` but still counts as one of the page's dates. `next_before` is the last date of the page when more posted dates remain, else null. `no_friends` lists no days (the app shows the person's own diary there).
6. **Day labels:** `today` is the day in play, `yesterday` is the civil date before it, everything else `earlier` (plain `YYYY-MM-DD` arithmetic, no zone, no `Date`). `day_label` is `last_night` when `LateWindow`, else `today`.
7. **Locked card data:** `friends_posted` is the number of friends with a posted day for the day in play; `names` is up to four first names of friends who have an ENTRY on that day, in the order they posted; `category` and `action` come from `BoardFacts`.
8. **One visibility function.** The single post read builds `social.Facts` with `social.Service.ViewFacts` and calls `social.CanView`; the board list queries filter by the same three conditions in SQL, and a hand-written matrix test (Task 2) proves the list and the post read agree on every viewer and entry of a small world, in every Board state.
9. **Photo links** come from `board.PhotoLinks.URL(facts social.Facts, key string) (string, bool)`: it returns `("", false)` when `CanView(facts)` is false and never calls the store then; otherwise `EntryPhotoStore.PublicURL(key)`. It is the only code of Phase 5 that calls `PublicURL` (a grep proves it). The links are public to anyone who has them until the real storage account exists; the key never leaves the server.
10. **Reactions.** `reaction` must pass `spin.IsReaction`. Set is an upsert on `(entry_id, user_id)` (one row; a change updates `reaction` and `updated_at`; `created_at` keeps the first time), clear deletes the row, both idempotent. The owner of the entry gets `ErrOwnPost` (409 `own_post`). A post the caller cannot see is `ErrNotFound`.
11. **Comments.** `CleanComment`: trimmed; `\r\n` becomes `\n`; 1 to 140 runes by rune count; no control characters except `\n`; reasons `required`, `too_long`, `invalid` on field `text`. Allowed iff `social.CanComment`. Delete is allowed to the writer and to the post's owner only; anyone else, and a missing id, is `ErrNotFound`. Comments written by a person who has a block with the viewer (either way) are not returned to the viewer and are not counted.
12. **The post page** (`GET /v1/posts/{id}`) returns the card, the author's note (`entries.comment`), the comments oldest first (each with its `at_label`), `can_react`, `can_comment`, and for the owner only `received` (the reactions of people the owner has no block with, newest change first). Phase 5 always sends a number for `comment_count` and a list for `comments`; Phase 6 may send null to a stranger.
13. **Phase 4's `friends`** becomes `social.Service.FriendCount` through a one-method interface injected into Phase 4's summary code; a nil interface keeps the constant 0 so Phase 4's own tests do not need social.
14. **Time.** No `time.Now()` in new code. A reaction or comment is stamped with `clock.Clock.NowFor(actorID)`, read once per call.
15. **Reset.** The caller's reactions and comments are cleared by a resetter registered on plan 05-01's `POST /v1/test/reset-social` (listed throwaway accounts only); the Phase 2 and 3 reset is not edited. Photo files and entries are not touched here.
16. **Time labels are written by the server, by pure helpers in `internal/spin/labels.go`** (next to `window.go` and `week.go`; no `time.Now()`; zones from `ParseZone`):
    - A card's `posted_local_time` is the time of day of the entry's `created_at` in the AUTHOR's own zone, "8:41 PM": the zone the entry's spin was made in (`spins.tz_name`, the same choice Phase 2 makes for `spun_local_time`), parsed with `ParseZone`. A name that fails to parse falls back to the viewer's zone and never fails the page.
    - A comment's `at_label` is written in the VIEWER's zone (`BoardFacts.Zone`, with `BoardFacts.Now`) by ONE rule, `spin.ViewerLabel`, shared with plan 05-03's notification items: the time of day ("8:52 PM") for an instant on the viewer's current local day (an instant after it, from a moved test clock, counts as today), the short weekday ("Mon") for the six local days before, the month and day ("Oct 3", no leading zero, no year) before that. The day count is plain civil-date arithmetic on `YYYY-MM-DD` strings, never "24 hours ago".
    - The phone formats no time and has no fallback formatter.
</binding_decisions>
