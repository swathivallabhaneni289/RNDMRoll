<binding_decisions>
Final.
1. **No migration, no stored events, no jobs.** The list is built when asked. The one table is part C of migration 0006 (written by plan 05-01 Task 2a): `notification_state (user_id uuid primary key references users(id) on delete cascade, seen_at timestamptz not null)`. Nothing is added to `users`.
2. **Sources and items.** Each person's list holds at most these, within the last 30 days:
   - `reaction`: a row of `post_reactions` on an entry the person owns, by someone else. `at` is the row's `updated_at`; `id` is `reaction:<entry_id>:<actor_id>`; `actor` the reactor; `entry_id`; `category_name` as written (from the entry's spin); `reaction` the name.
   - `comment`: a row of `post_comments` on an entry the person owns, by someone else. `at` is `created_at`; `id` is `comment:<comment_id>`; `actor`; `entry_id`; `category_name`; `text` the comment cut to 60 runes with three ASCII dots added when it was longer (line breaks become spaces).
   - `friend_request`: a pending row of `friend_requests` addressed to the person. `at` is `created_at`; `id` is `friend_request:<request_id>`; `actor` the asker; `request_id`.
   - `friend_accepted`: a `friendships` row whose `requested_by` is the person (someone accepted their request). `at` is the row's `created_at`; `id` is `friend_accepted:<other_id>`; `actor` the other person.
   - `spin_open`: ONE item, only while the person's local time is at or after 20:00 and before local midnight: `at` is that date's 20:00 instant; `id` is `spin_open:<date>`; no actor.
   - `bonus_spin`: a `goal_weeks` row with `bonus_granted` true and `goal_met_at` inside the window. `at` is `goal_met_at`; `id` is `bonus_spin:<week_start>`; `text` is the weekly goal as a decimal string (the app writes "You hit {text} this week. One bonus spin added.").
3. **Exclusions.** Items about a person the viewer has a block with (either way) are left out; a person's own reactions and comments on their own posts are left out; a deleted post's items are gone (cascade).
4. **Order and cap.** Newest `at` first, ties by `id` descending, at most 50 items after merging. Each source query is limited to 50.
5. **Sections** are computed on the server in the person's stored zone from civil dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`), with `n = spin.CivilDaysBetween(local date of the item, local date of now)`: `today` when `n <= 0`; `week` when `1 <= n <= 6` (the six dates before today); `earlier` when `n >= 7` (up to 30 days). An item dated after today (a moved test clock) counts as `today`.
6. **Unread.** An item is unread when its `at` is strictly after the person's seen time; with no seen row every item is unread. `unread` at the top of the list is true when any listed item is. There is no per-item read state.
7. **Seen.** `POST /v1/notifications/seen` sets `seen_at` to the person's `now` and never moves it backwards (`greatest(old, new)`). The page's own dots come from the list it loaded before it called `seen`. A tiny window in which an item created between the two calls is marked seen unseen is accepted.
8. **The bell's check** (`GET /v1/notifications/summary`) uses `exists` queries per source against `greatest(seen_at, now - 30 days)` plus the two derived items; it must agree with the list's top-level `unread` (a property test).
9. **Time.** No `time.Now()` in new code. `now` comes from `clock.Clock.NowFor(viewerID)` through the spin slice's notification facts, once per call. All three routes need the viewer's zone; a missing zone is 400 field `time_zone` as for Today.
10. **Reset.** `ClearFor` deletes the caller's seen row and is registered as a resetter on plan 05-01's `POST /v1/test/reset-social`.
11. **`at_label`** (decided with the coordinator on 2026-10-10: the server writes it). Every item carries `at_label`, written by `spin.ViewerLabel(at, facts.Now, facts.Zone)` in the VIEWER's zone: the time of day ("9:18 PM") for an instant on the viewer's current local day, the short weekday ("Mon") for the six local days before, the month and day ("Oct 3", no leading zero, no year) before that. It is the same helper and the same day count as a comment's `at_label` in plan 05-02 (decision 16 there), and it is the same count as the item's `section`: `today` items read as a time of day, `week` items as a weekday, `earlier` items as a month and day. It is always a non-empty string. The notification code writes no time itself.
</binding_decisions>
